What I found

01

Your biggest audience is on YouTube, not on your site.

Your Nightrod walk-around video had 9,641,855 views when I checked on October 15, 2026. Your homepage has no video on it, and only 2 of your 99 build pages (Tank and Ambush) play one. The video's description also asks viewers to email a Gmail address instead of using your site.

02

Your build pages don't ask for anything.

I checked all 99 build pages. None has an inquiry or contact button, and 82 of them show little more than the build's name and photos. Most of your for-sale pages do have an INQUIRE NOW button, so the build pages are where people with a custom idea hit a dead end.

03

Calling takes extra steps.

The homepage never shows your phone number. The contact page shows it as plain text, not a tap-to-call link. The contact page does have a message form, and it asks for a phone number, but I didn't submit it.

04

Your address and hours tell two stories.

The contact page says 4300 W Tropicana Ave, by appointment only. The business data behind the page says 7465 Dean Martin Drive, Suite 103, and lists every day as open 00:00 to 00:00, which reads as around the clock. The Tropicana address is the one other websites repeat.

05

Search results and shared links lead with a one-word title.

Your homepage title is just “DEVINDIEGODESIGNS”, with no “Las Vegas” or “V-Rod” in it. Your page description is good, but 69 of your 99 build pages have no description at all.

What I checked on your site

  • Found
    Not blocking AI crawlers

    Your robots.txt (the standard Squarespace file) names 29 AI and data crawlers, including GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider and Amazonbot. They follow the same rules as every other visitor, and those rules only cover your admin, search, account and API pages. I tested 11 well-known crawler names (GPTBot, ChatGPT-User, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot) and none is blocked from your pages.

  • Found
    Structured data describes the business

    The homepage includes business markup with your name, phone (702) 800-0483, address, and links to your Facebook, Instagram and YouTube. The phone number matches the one on your contact page.

  • Gap
    Address and hours don't match

    The markup lists 7465 Dean Martin Drive, Suite 103, Las Vegas, NV 89139 and open every day from 00:00 to 00:00. Your contact page says 4300 W Tropicana Ave and by appointment only. Search tools that read both may repeat either one.

  • Gap
    No tap-to-call link

    I found no phone link on the homepage or the contact page. I checked the page code in a desktop browser, so how a phone treats the plain number is unverified.

  • Gap
    No Google listing link

    I found no link or embedded map pointing to your Google Maps listing on the homepage or the contact page.

  • Gap
    Homepage title is one word

    The title tag is “DEVINDIEGODESIGNS”. The description underneath is good: it says you are a Las Vegas custom vehicle builder known for black V-Rods.

  • Note
    Most build pages are photos only

    Of 99 build pages, 82 show little more than a name and photos, 69 have no page description, and 2 play a video. Search tools have very little text to quote for most of your builds.

  • Note
    Placeholder pages sit in your sitemap

    Your sitemap lists pages named “Leads”, “Waitlist”, “HOME”, “404” and “131 Breakout (Copy)”. Search tools read the sitemap, so they can see these too.

  • Gap
    No llms.txt file

    /llms.txt returns “not found”. It is a newer, optional file that some AI tools read to understand a site, so this is a small gap.

These are plain checks anyone can repeat on October 15, 2026: what the site publishes, not a score.

What I couldn't measure

  • Rankings in Google or Google Maps.
  • What ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI answers say when asked directly. I didn't ask those assistants myself, so the searches above are a stand-in.
